# Canvas Design

## Print Specifications

- **A3 format**: 297×420mm
- **Resolution**: 300 DPI for print, 72 DPI for digital
- **Color mode**: CMYK for print, RGB for digital
- **Bleed**: 3mm bleed on all sides for print production
- **Safe zone**: 10mm inset from trim edge

## Poster Layout

- **Hierarchy**: headline (20%), visual (50%), body text (20%), footer (10%)
- **Margins**: generous whitespace around all elements
- **Grid**: single-column or two-column depending on density
- **Visual**: centered hero image or graphic

## Digital Canvas

- **Workshop slides**: 1920×1080 (16:9) or 1024×768 (4:3)
- **Social media**: 1200×630 for Open Graph, 1080×1080 for Instagram
- **Presentation**: consistent margins, brand header/footer
